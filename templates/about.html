About MZPets

Caring for animals since 2011.

Our Story

MZPets was founded with a simple mission: give every animal the love, care, and home it deserves. What started as a small neighbourhood pet shop has grown into a trusted destination for pet owners across the city.

Our team of passionate animal lovers works tirelessly to source the best products, deliver expert grooming, and facilitate responsible adoptions.

Our Values

  • Animal welfare above everything
  • Responsible breeding and adoption
  • Premium, ethically‑sourced products
  • Community education and outreach
  • Sustainability in everything we do
